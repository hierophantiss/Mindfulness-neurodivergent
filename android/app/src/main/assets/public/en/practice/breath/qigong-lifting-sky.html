Lifting the Sky (Qigong) – Vitality & Extension

Guided breathwork: Lifting the Sky (Qigong). Vitality & Extension. A neurodivergent-friendly breathing exercise for ADHD and autism nervous system regulation.

Simple Qigong exercise that stretches the whole body, opens the chest and brings oxygen to the lungs.

Inhale: push the sky (6s)

Hold: extend (2s)

Exhale: lower arms (6s)
