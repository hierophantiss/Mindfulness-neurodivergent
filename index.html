Neurodivergent Awareness

A trauma-informed mindfulness guide specifically designed for neurodivergent minds (ADHD, Autism). The application offers a comprehensive path to self-regulation through more than 100 specialized exercises and a structured 8-week program.

The Fourfold Axis

  • Body/Gravity (Σώμα): Grounding and physical presence.
  • Breath (Ανάσα): Balancing the nervous system through rhythmic breathing.
  • Attention (Προσοχή): Training the observer mind.
  • Space (Χώρος): Cultivating expansive, non-judgmental awareness.

Key Offerings

  • 8-Week Guided Program: A progressive journey from basics to mastery.
  • Invisible Microdoses: Quick, discreet mindfulness for everyday life.
  • The Rabbit Hole: Deep dives into philosophical and clinical concepts.
  • Digital Journal: A personal space for reflection and tracking growth.
  • The Sanctuary: Ambient soundscapes for deep rest and recovery.
