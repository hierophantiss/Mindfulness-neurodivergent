Neurodivergent Mindfulness

A trauma-informed mindfulness guide specifically designed for neurodivergent minds (ADHD, Autism). The practice is built around the Fourfold Axis:

  • Body (Σώμα/Βαρύτητα): Grounding in physical presence and the sensation of gravity.
  • Breath (Ανάσα): Using the natural rhythm of breathing as an anchor.
  • Attention (Προσοχή): Developing the ability to consciously direct and hold focus.
  • Space (Χώρος): Cultivating open awareness and the realization that you are the space in which experiences occur.
